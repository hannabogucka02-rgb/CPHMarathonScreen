CHARITY
Copenhagen Marathon is partnered with realbuzz, experts in connecting runners with charity organisations all over the world. This means you'll be supported throughout your journey, so you can focus on your training and fundraising.
4 reasons to run for charity
  1. You raise awareness for an important cause
  2. You get extra motivation for your training
  3. You secure a Copenhagen Marathon race entry when you hit your target
  4. You inspire others to do the same
So join the team and get started today. We look forward to hearing from you.
How it works
  • Select a charity through the QR code
  • The charity will be in touch directly about joining their team
  • Once you're on a team, set up your fundraising page and start spreading the word
You'll get plenty of tips and support for your fundraising and of course, you'll secure a race entry for the Copenhagen Marathon - even when the event is officially sold out.
