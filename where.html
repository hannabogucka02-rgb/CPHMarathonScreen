START & FINISH LOCATION

The iconic Copenhagen Marathon starts and finishes on Øster Allé, right next to the historic Fælledparken in the Østerbro district.

The race village opens early on race morning for runner check-in, bag drop, and warm-up zones. Spectators have massive open viewing areas along Øster Allé and the surrounding park perimeter to catch both the grand start and the emotional final sprint!
