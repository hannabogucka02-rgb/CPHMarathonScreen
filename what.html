ABOUT THE EVENT & KEY DETAILS

Welcome to one of Europe's most iconic road races! Established in 1980, the Copenhagen Marathon brings together thousands of runners and spectators from all over the world.

Runners pass through historic neighborhoods including Inner City, Østerbro, Nørrebro, Vesterbro, and Christianshavn—framed by scenic lakes, royal palaces, and vibrant street crowds.
